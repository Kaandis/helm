Where you already are

Start dragging in Finder or on the desktop and hold a key. No app to open, no window to find.

No permissions, offline

What’s on the wheels

⇧ Convert

Photos to JPG, PNG, HEIC, PDF or TIFF. PDF pages to PNG or JPG. Videos to MP4, MOV, GIF or their sound. Audio to M4A, WAV or AIFF.

⌘ Tools

Compress, halve or clean photos, remove the background, copy their text, make one PDF, read a QR code. Merge, split, compress or copy the text of PDFs. Compress or mute videos. Extract archives.

⌃ Actions

Cut, AirDrop, copy the path, Mail, zip, open Terminal there, or copy a picture to paste it anywhere.

Questions

Doesn’t holding ⌘ move the file?

Only if you drop it in another Finder folder. Dropped on Helm’s wheel, the file is only read; the original stays where it was, and the result lands next to it.

What permissions does Helm need?

None. It notices a drag from the system’s drag pasteboard and the modifier keys, which any app may read. No Accessibility, Screen Recording or Full Disk Access.

Will it get in the way of other apps?

A menu opens only when you shake a file drag or hold a wheel key during one. It never opens in full-screen apps, you can turn it off for apps whose drag shortcuts clash, and you can change the keys or pause Helm from the menu bar.

How does Cut work?

Drop files on Cut and they wait on a small shelf in the corner, untouched. Drag the shelf into any folder to move them there, or press ⌥⌘V in that folder. Close the shelf and nothing moves.

Does anything go to the cloud?

No. Removing backgrounds and reading text use the models already on your Mac, the same ones Preview and Photos use. Helm has no account and sends nothing anywhere.

Dragging from Photos doesn’t open a wheel.

Photos and Mail drag promised files that only exist once dropped. Drag them to the desktop first, then use Helm on them.

macOS says it can’t be opened.

Builds aren’t notarized yet. Right-click Helm and choose Open, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Helm.app.
